Stary Nova DayZ

Stary Sobor and Novy Sobor, collectively known as the "Stary/Novy" area, make up one of the most popular spots on the map and have been for years. This area gets its reputation from being the most versatile location on the map, sitting right in the smack-dab middle alongside multiple open fields. From this spot, you are moments away from the Northwest Airfield (NWAF), medical centers, and numerous helicopter crashes that tend to yield some of the best official server gear in the game. Whether you want to be a farmer, a combat soldier, a base builder, or just live to explore, this area offers the very best of DayZ's sandbox features. Popular nearby hotspots include Radio Zenit and Kabanino. When it comes to DayZ survival, these towns provide some of the most intense action on official servers. Aside from heading straight into the Northwest Airfield, this is the go-to spot to experience a mix of everything the game is about. Since the DayZ 1.29 release date, this part of the map has become even more popular due to the latest influx of players searching for the new clothing and weapons being tested at crashes and military areas.
