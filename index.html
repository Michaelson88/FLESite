Welcome to Flesh Line Extreme

Lazy Dayz

Dayz is a hard game for any beginner, but what happens after the new phase wears off? Bohemia developed Dayz, which turned out to become a masterpiece after 13 years. After enough time and playthrough on official or community the runs can start to seem a bit stale or even sometimes boring. After enough experience you start to know where you are at all times on the map and then you start to know exactly how and where to get your favorite weapons/gear. We can fix that problem. Click on the server details at the top to get straight into the action or continue to read to find out what this server is about.

This Dayz server will consume you

Instead of making a community like 95% of the other people, we have dedicated this server to be for the experienced players. The more experienced players that populate a map the more challenging the game becomes, giving you that old but familiar taste of excitment on what ifs. It will set an entirely different mood on pvp. This server will aim to make even the most experienced players in dayz question the moves they make and wondering if they made the right decision again and again. Below we tell you what we have done to make the server harder. Dayz loot has been reduced by a whopping 75%. You read that correct Seventy Five Percent!. We do this to make sure that only the strong and smart in this world survive. This server is set to test you and eventually break most of you. The best fit for this community will love a real challenge every single day they play. Weather is set to eastern central. Prepare for the winter as the ground will freez, prepare for the true 10 year post apocalypse. As shoes and clothes will almost be rags.

Dynamic Events VS Loot Runs

Food is the priority in any survival game and we aim to put this at the forefront of this server. Finding food is the number one scare in Flesh Line Extreme while PVP is a close second. Weapons will remain scattered throughout the map. We have placed at the very least one of EVERY weapon in the game.
